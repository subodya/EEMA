## Brand & Style

This design system channels an understated, high-end tropical minimalism tailored for an artisanal Sri Lankan fashion atelier. It embodies tactile organic luxury, effortless resort-wear elegance, and bespoke craftsmanship.

### Target Audience & Emotional Tone
Designed for discerning domestic and global patrons seeking authentic sustainable luxury—pure handloomed linens, bespoke artisanal batik expressions, and precision-tailored silhouettes. The interface evokes stillness, high editorial curation, and tactile warmth. The mood eliminates the aggressive friction of high-volume mass-market e-commerce, cultivating instead the intimate bespoke ambiance of private salon appointments and direct concierge relationships.

### Design Movement: High-End Editorial Minimalism
The aesthetic strictly implements high-typicality editorial minimalism:
- **Zero-Radius Discipline:** Razor-sharp rectangular edges across all image viewports, containers, buttons, and callouts, reflecting architectural linen draping and tailored garment lines.
- **Organic Earth Canvas:** Layered ecru and warm natural unbleached linen tones replacing harsh synthetic whites.
- **Direct Concierge Commerce:** Replaces standard impersonal shopping carts with personalized, high-touch luxury touchpoints ("Inquire on WhatsApp", "Consult via Concierge").
- **Signature Floral Accent:** Restrained, intentional flashes of deep rose berry drawn directly from the emblem spiral, punctuating key badges and bespoke discovery states against timeless noir typography.

## Colors

The color palette reflects natural raw fibers, earth dyes, and botanical pigments.

### Functional Palette Roles
- **Primary (`#B8236B`):** The signature emblem rose berry. Used sparingly for curated artisanal badges, editorial accents, bespoke consultation focus rings, and high-priority action markers.
- **Secondary (`#1A1A1A`):** Deep editorial noir. Anchors primary luxury headlines, dominant call-to-action buttons, and architectural dividers.
- **Tertiary (`#8C7A6B`):** Warm raw stone and coconut husk tone. Serves for subtle collection counters, metadata labels, and garment material tags.
- **Neutral (`#FBF9F5`):** Raw unbleached linen ecru. Acts as the warm, eye-comfort foundation for all viewports and page backgrounds.

### Specialized Shades
- **Surface Elevation (`#F5F1EA` & `#EAE4DC`):** Used for card backing plates, lookbook frames, and input field strokes to preserve low-contrast tactile layering without cold gray shadows.
- **Concierge Channel (`#1E5E3A`):** A muted, heritage forest green reserved exclusively for direct artisan WhatsApp inquiry actions, retaining luxury dignity rather than hyper-saturated neon messaging.

## Typography

The typographical pairing expresses the contrast between traditional artisanal craft and contemporary modern utility.

### Hierarchy & Composition
- **Headlines (Playfair Display):** High-contrast transitional serif delivering classical prestige, bespoke couture tailoring tone, and editorial grace. Large display sizes utilize gentle negative tracking to keep titling tight and magazine-like.
- **Body & Functional Microcopy (Inter):** Neutral, utilitarian sans-serif set in light-to-regular weights with generous line heights to evoke calmness and legibility across fabric spec sheets and measurement guides.
- **Labels & Micro-headers (Inter All-Caps):** Key navigation items, category divisions, craft badges, and garment tags always appear in 11px uppercase with wide `0.15em` letter spacing, establishing structural clarity and restraint.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is grounded in a spacious, 12-column editorial grid inspired by high-fashion print lookbooks.

### Grid & Canvas Structure
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12-column fluid grid, `3.5rem` outer margins, `1.5rem` gutters. Product listings utilize 2-column, 3-column, or asymmetrical 4-column groupings punctuated by large, full-height editorial story blocks spanning 6 columns.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 6-column fluid grid, `2rem` outer margins, `1rem` gutters. Lookbook features stack above dual-item product displays.
- **Mobile (Below 768px):** 2-column or single-column continuous grid, `1.25rem` outer canvas padding, `0.75rem` internal gutters allowing imagery to dominate screen real estate.

### Spacing Philosophy
Whitespace is treated as an active luxury material. Generous vertical pauses (`space-2xl` and `space-3xl`) isolate sections, ensuring each bespoke collection and artisanal batik feature breathes without visual crowding.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved strictly through delicate tonal framing, hairline rules, and surface layering—never through synthetic dropped shadows.

### The Pure Flat & Tonal Stack
- **Surface Layering:** Elevated cards and modal surfaces sit on unbleached linen tiers (`#F5F1EA`), resting flat against the primary background (`#FBF9F5`).
- **Hairline Boundaries:** Crisp, 1px structural borders tinted in `#EAE4DC` establish boundary definition without visual clutter.
- **Zero Heavy Shadows:** Standard dropped shadows are strictly prohibited. Floating concierge elements and sticky inquiry bars utilize a 1px top boundary in `#EAE4DC` and an imperceptible micro-blur tint (`rgba(26, 26, 26, 0.04)`) to retain crisp architectural presence.

## Shapes

The design system adheres exclusively to a strict zero-radius (`0px`) geometry.

### Angular Precision
Every visual artifact—from product cards, inquiry buttons, editorial photo blocks, and dropdown menus to craft chips—maintains unsoftened, 90-degree corners. This reflects the crisp edge of handwoven bolts of linen, bespoke pattern drafting, and editorial gallery aesthetics. The circular emblem flower serves as the sole organic circular entity, intentionally highlighted by the rigid rectilinear world framing it.

## Components

### Action Buttons
- **Direct Concierge Action (WhatsApp):** Sharp rectangular button styled in `#1A1A1A` with high-contrast `#FFFFFF` text or framed in `#1E5E3A`. Hover transitions subtly reveal the rose accent `#B8236B`. Label: `INQUIRE ON WHATSAPP`.
- **Secondary Outlined Action:** 1px hairline stroke in `#1A1A1A`, transparent background, charcoal text with `0.15em` letter spacing. Label: `CONSULT VIA ATELIER`.
- **Interactive States:** No rounded pills; state changes rely on solid black fill inversions and smooth background transitions.

### Product & Editorial Lookbook Cards
- **Visual Ratio:** 3:4 and 4:5 vertical portrait formats honoring full silhouette drape.
- **Image Presentation:** Edge-to-edge imagery with 0px corner radii. Minimalist overlay reveals alternate drape or hand-embroidery macro zoom on hover.
- **Card Metadata:** Left-aligned or centered underneath image: garment name in `headline-sm`, pricing in `price-numeral`, and available custom tailoring options in uppercase `label-caps`.

### Artisanal Badges & Craft Chips
- **Aesthetic:** Rectangular badges with a subtle 1px border in `#B8236B` or filled with pale rose blush (`#F8E9F0`). Text set in deep berry (`#8C154E`) or noir (`#1A1A1A`).
- **Examples:** `HANDMADE LINEN`, `ARTISANAL BATIK`, `BESPOKE TAILORED`.

### Inquiry Fields & Direct Messaging Cards
- **Form Controls:** Underlined or framed in 1px `#EAE4DC` tone. Focused state shifts to solid `#1A1A1A`. 
- **Typography:** Placeholder text in Inter regular 13px; labels in Inter uppercase 11px.
- **Custom Measurement Panel:** Modular tabbed panels enabling customers to submit bust, waist, and length measurements prior to launching their direct WhatsApp order thread.

### Global Header & Navigation
- **Architecture:** Clean, low-height linen bar (`#FBF9F5`) with centered serif brand mark, flanked by minimal uppercase navigation links and direct concierge contact action. Sticky bar utilizes a single bottom 1px stroke in `#EAE4DC`.
