Ready-to-wear pieces

If a ready-to-wear piece doesn't fit, you can exchange it for another size within 7 days of delivery.

  • The piece must be unworn and in its original condition.
  • Exchanges are for a different size of the same piece.

Made-to-measure pieces

Made-to-measure pieces are cut to your own measurements, so they can't be cancelled or exchanged once made. Instead, every made-to-measure piece comes with complimentary alterations.

  • If your piece needs fine-tuning when it arrives, our Colombo studio will alter it at no cost.

How to request an exchange or alteration

  1. 01

    Message us on WhatsApp with your name, the piece you ordered, and the size you'd like, or what needs adjusting.

  2. 02

    For alterations, include a photo of the fit if you can. It helps our cutter see exactly what to change.

  3. 03

    Our team will confirm the next steps with you directly.

Questions?

We're happy to help with sizing before you order, too. Our size guide and made-to-measure service are a good place to start.
