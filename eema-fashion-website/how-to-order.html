Delivery & payment

Delivery

Islandwide by courier in 2–4 business days. Delivery is free on orders over Rs 10,000.

Made-to-measure pieces take 3–5 working days to make before they are sent.

Payment

Pay by bank transfer from any major Sri Lankan bank (Commercial Bank, Sampath, HNB), or cash on delivery.

Returns & exchanges

Ready-to-wear

Exchange for another size within 7 days of delivery, if unworn and in original condition.

Made to measure

Cut for you, so these can't be cancelled or exchanged, but alterations are free.
